**Where the eight internet pictures come from**

These pictures are a small demo for the presentation, not a test set. Eight pictures are far too few to measure a model.

All eight come from Wikimedia Commons, where they are shared under free licenses. For this project each one was cropped to the face and resized to 224 x 224 pixels. The models see them the same way they see the dataset: in grayscale at 48 x 48 pixels.

The emotion of each picture is the one its author set out to show, or the one its Commons page describes. Nobody measured it. The fear and sad pictures are two faces cut from one sheet of seven posed expressions by the same person; which face shows which emotion was judged by eye.
