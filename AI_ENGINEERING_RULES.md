My AI Engineering Rules

Never blindly copy code.
Understand every important line, parameter, function, and design decision before accepting an implementation.

Attempt before asking.
Think through problems independently before requesting help. Develop a theory, even if it turns out to be wrong.

AI explains, I verify.
Never treat an AI explanation as unquestionable truth. Verify important claims through documentation, mathematics, experiments, or tests.

Break what I build.
Change parameters, introduce edge cases, remove components, and observe the consequences.
No unexplained dependencies.
Understand what important libraries, methods, and external components do and why they are being used.

Periodically work without AI.
Reimplement important concepts from memory. Debug problems independently. Write small programs without assistance.

Never confuse finishing with learning.
A working project does not automatically mean I understand the project.
Experiment beyond the tutorial.
Don't stop when I've reproduced something. Change it, investigate it, and discover what happens.
Understand decisions, not just syntax.
Know why an approach was chosen, what alternatives exist, and what its limitations are.
AI accelerates my learning; it does not replace my thinking.
